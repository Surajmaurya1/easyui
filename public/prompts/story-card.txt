Declared props:
- `cards` (StoryCard[]); default: undefined — Array of story cards containing title, image, description, and optional link information
- `initialIndex` (number); default: 0 — Index of the story displayed initially
- `loop` (boolean); default: true — Enables endless circular scrolling through the cards
- `enableWheelScroll` (boolean); default: true — Allows trackpad or mouse wheel gestures to scroll through cards
- `autoPlay` (boolean); default: false — Automatically advances through the story cards
- `autoPlayInterval` (number); default: 5000 — Time in milliseconds between automatic story transitions
- `showNavigation` (boolean); default: true — Controls whether previous and next navigation buttons are displayed
- `title` (string); default: "What's happening" — Custom title heading for the story cards section
- `subtitle` (string); default: "Discover the latest stories." — Custom subtitle for the story cards section
- `className` (string); default: undefined — Optional custom Tailwind styling for the component wrapper
